Does DoorDash Have a Student Discount? (November 2026)

Yes, DoorDash has a student discount, but it is not a code. It is DashPass for Students at $4.99 per month, verified with SheerID. The free 12 month sign-up ended October 12, 2026.

What Is the DoorDash Student Discount?

The DoorDash student discount is DashPass for Students at $4.99 per month or $48 per year, half the standard DashPass price. It is a discounted membership, not a typed promo code. Students get the same DashPass benefits as regular members: $0 delivery fees on eligible orders, reduced service fees, 5% back in DoorDash credit on eligible pickup orders, and member only promotions. Benefits apply only to eligible orders from DashPass merchants that meet the merchant subtotal minimum.

How Much Does Student DashPass Cost?

Student DashPass costs $4.99 per month. The standard DashPass costs $9.99 per month or $96 per year. The student annual price is reported at $48 per year, so check the live DashPass page for the current annual price before you join.

How Do I Verify My Student Status?

Verify your student status with SheerID, DoorDash's verification partner. SheerID asks for your name, date of birth, school, and student email address. If automatic verification fails, upload a school document such as a transcript, class schedule, or tuition receipt. Manual review usually takes up to 24 hours, and SheerID sends an email when it is finished. Only college and university students at an eligible university can verify. High school students are not covered by the official student plan.

Is Student DashPass Free?

No, Student DashPass is not free now. The free 12 month sign-up ran from August 17, 2026 through October 12, 2026, and that window has closed. The live offer now is the regular Student DashPass plan at $4.99 per month. If you signed up during the free window, your 12 months run from your sign-up date, then move to $4.99 per month automatically.

What Happens After My Free Year or After Graduation?

After your free year ends, your plan moves to Student DashPass at $4.99 per month automatically, while you are still a student. After graduation, when SheerID can no longer confirm your student status, your plan moves to standard DashPass at $9.99 per month automatically. DoorDash sends an email before each change, and you can cancel any time before the change date to avoid being charged. Cancelling ends your DashPass benefits at the end of the month of cancellation.

How Can Students Save Without a Student Code?

Students save without a student code through the Offers tab, the checkout Promotions panel, group orders, pickup, and gift cards. Follow this routine in order, and check each place before you pay.

1

Open the Offers tab in the DoorDash app and check the offers for your address.

2

Open checkout and check Available Rewards and Promotions before you place the order.

3

Start a group order with friends to split delivery and service fees and meet minimums more easily.

4

Choose pickup when you can, because there is no delivery fee, and DashPass members get 5% back in credit on eligible pickup orders.

5

Check discounted gift cards in the app and at warehouse clubs before you order.

Is Student DashPass Worth It?

Yes, Student DashPass is worth it if you order at least 1 eligible order per month, because the delivery fee saved on one order is usually $3.99 to $5.99, close to the $4.99 monthly price. At 2 orders per month it clearly pays for itself. It is usually not worth it if you order less than once a month, order mostly for pickup, or order below the merchant minimum. Students who already get free DashPass through Chase, T-Mobile, Lyft, or Chegg should not pay for Student DashPass as well, because the benefits do not stack.

Can I combine a student discount with another promo code?

No, only 1 code works per order. Student DashPass benefits apply automatically where they are eligible, and a code cannot be combined with another promo code.
