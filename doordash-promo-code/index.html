DoorDash Promo Codes for Existing Users

Existing users rarely get a public sitewide code, and most savings come from targeted codes, the Offers tab and DashPass. See the full guide for existing users: DoorDash promo codes for existing users.

How Do You Enter a DoorDash Promo Code?

1

Add your items to the cart and go to checkout.

2

Find Enter Promo Code, or open Promo codes, rewards and gift cards.

3

Enter the code exactly as shown. DoorDash codes are case sensitive.

4

Tap Apply, then check the order total before you tap Place Order.

5

If it fails, read the condition message. It usually shows the missing minimum or required item.

Gift cards are different. Do not enter a gift card code in the promo code box. Gift cards have their own redeem field.

What DoorDash Deals Work Without a Promo Code?

$0 delivery on first order

New customers can get $0 delivery fee on their first DoorDash order when they sign up. This offer is shown on DoorDash's official promos page and does not require a typed code. Service fees, taxes and tip can still apply.

In-app Offers tab

Open the app, enter your delivery address, and open the Offers tab. These offers are funded by merchants, applied automatically when you meet the terms, and vary by address and restaurant. They rotate often, so a copied list can go stale within days.

Merchant promotions

Many restaurants run their own DoorDash promotions for new, existing or lapsed customers. These are usually priced into the cart or shown in the app, not entered as a code. Check the restaurant page in the app first.

Is DashPass Worth It for Discounts?

DashPass is DoorDash's membership program. Members get $0 delivery fees, reduced service fees and member only promotions on eligible orders at participating merchants. Participating merchants show a DashPass icon in the app, and you must meet that merchant's subtotal minimum for the benefit to apply. The saving is applied automatically at checkout.

DashPass is most useful if you order often from participating merchants and regularly meet the minimum. DashPass pricing is widely reported as $9.99 per month or $96 per year, but that price was not stated on the official help page found during this research, so check the live Get DashPass page for the current price before you join. A free trial is only for users who have not tried DashPass before, it auto renews unless you cancel, and cancelling during a trial ends the benefits immediately.

Do Partner Offers Change DoorDash Prices?

Some card and carrier plans include DashPass or DoorDash benefits. These offers are controlled by the issuer, and their terms, deadlines and eligibility change. Do not rely on a summary on a coupon page for a partner offer. Open the issuer's own terms page and confirm the benefit, the activation step and the end date before you count it as a saving.

DoorDash Promo Code FAQs

How do I enter a DoorDash promo code?

Go to checkout, open Enter Promo Code or Promo codes, rewards and gift cards, enter the code exactly as shown, tap Apply, and check the total before you place the order.

Can I use two DoorDash promo codes on one order?

No. Only one promo code can be used per order. Entering a second code replaces the first one.

Why is my DoorDash promo code not working?

The most common reasons are account targeting, a minimum spend that is not met on subtotal, a code that was already used, an excluded restaurant or item, or an expired offer. Check the condition message at checkout.

Do DoorDash promo codes work for existing users?

Yes, but existing users rarely get a public sitewide code. Most existing user savings come from the Offers tab, merchant promotions and DashPass benefits.

How do I get free delivery on DoorDash without a code?

New customers can get $0 delivery fee on their first order when they sign up. Existing customers can get $0 delivery through DashPass on eligible orders, or through a merchant offer in the Offers tab.

Is DashPass worth it just for discounts?

It can be worth it if you order often from participating merchants and meet the subtotal minimum. Check the live DashPass price first, because the saving must be larger than the membership cost for your order pattern.

Can I use a DoorDash promo code on alcohol or grocery orders?

It depends on the code terms. Alcohol is excluded from many first-order codes, while grocery deals often come through merchant offers and the Offers tab. Always check the terms at checkout.

What happens to my promo code if I cancel my order?

The code is lost. DoorDash states that a promotion code is for one-time use and cannot be applied to another order, even if you cancelled the original order.

Does DoorDash publish promo codes on its promos page?

No. The official promos page at doordash.com/promos shows offer categories and asks for your address to find promos near you. It does not publish a list of typed codes.

Can I reuse a DoorDash promo code?

No. Each code can only be used once per account.

Does DoorDash have a student discount?

Student discount is covered in full on our student page, including DashPass student eligibility and current pricing. See: DoorDash student discount.

What is a DoorDash referral code?

Referral code is covered in full on our referral page, including who can use it and the current terms. See: DoorDash referral code.

Are there DoorDash promo codes for Taco Bell or McDonald's?

There is no stable typed code for one restaurant. Taco Bell, McDonald's and other restaurants run offers through the Offers tab and merchant promotions, and those offers vary by address and account. Open the restaurant page in the DoorDash app first, and check whether the code you want to use applies to that restaurant.
