DoorDash Referral Code - November 2026

What Is a DoorDash Referral Code? (November 2026)

A DoorDash referral code is not a typed code. It is a personal referral link in your account. The amount is shown only in your account and changes often, so check your account before you share it.

If you are looking for a code to enter at checkout, use the DoorDash promo code page instead.

How Does the DoorDash Referral Program Work?

The referral works in three steps. First, you share your personal referral link with someone you know. Second, that person signs up through your link and places a qualifying first order. Third, you get DoorDash credit toward a later order.

The referred person must be genuinely new. They must not have created a DoorDash account before, and they must not have used a phone number or a credit card that was linked to an earlier account. The referred person and the referrer must not be the same person, and both must be in the same country when the new account is created.

The qualifying order must meet the minimum subtotal shown in your referral link. That minimum is shown in your account, not on a public page. The new user's incentive applies automatically at checkout for their first order. Your credit usually appears within 24 hours of the qualifying order.

How Do I Find My DoorDash Referral Link?

Open your DoorDash app and go to Account, then tap Refer Friends. Your personal referral link is there. Share it directly with someone you know, by text or email, or use the Share Your Link option in the app.

Do not search for a referral code string to copy. DoorDash does not publish a public referral code. The link in your account is the only referral invite you have, and it belongs to you.

How Much Is the DoorDash Referral Bonus?

There is no fixed referral bonus. The amount is shown only in your account and it changes often. That is the official rule in the DoorDash Consumer Referral Program Terms, last updated May 1, 2023: the amount of Referral Credit varies and will be stated in the referrer's DoorDash account.

Published amounts conflict because they are snapshots from different accounts at different times. Some pages report $5, some report $10, and some report $10 off each of the first three orders. None of those figures is the current amount for every account. Check the amount shown in your own account before you share your link, and tell the person you invite to sign up right away, because the offer can change.

What Is the Difference Between a Referral Code and a Promo Code?

A promo code and a referral link are not the same thing. A promo code is a typed code you enter at checkout. A referral link is a personal invite you use at sign-up. You cannot enter a referral link as a promo code at checkout, and you cannot add a referral after you have already signed up.

Why Did I Not Get My Referral Credit?

The most common reason is that the new user was not actually new. If they had an account before, or if they used a phone number or a card that was linked to an earlier account, the referral does not qualify. The other common reasons are a missed minimum, a cancelled first order, or the 24 hour window not yet passing.

Check this list before you assume something went wrong:

  • Did the new user sign up through your link, not through the app on their own?
  • Did they place a qualifying first order that met the minimum shown in your link?
  • Was the order completed, not cancelled?
  • Have 24 hours passed since that order?
  • Have you reached your referral cap? In the United States and Canada the cap in the official terms is 10 qualified referrals. A DoorDash Caviar help page says 25, which conflicts with the terms, so check the cap shown in your own account.
  • Was your link posted on a public website? Posting it publicly can make the credit ineligible.

If all six points are clear and the credit still has not arrived, check the amount and the terms shown in your account, because the offer shown there is the one that applies to you.

Can I Share My Referral Link on a Website?

No. Share your referral link only with people you know personally. Posting it on a public website, a coupon site, social media, or a paid ad can make you ineligible, and the credit can be revoked. The official terms list public sites such as Google, Craigslist, Twitter, and RetailMeNot as examples.

This is also why this page lists no referral code or link to copy. A stranger's referral code is one person's personal invite, not a public code, and publishing it can disqualify the person who owns it.

How Does Dasher Referral Work?

Dasher referral is a separate program for drivers, not for customers. A Dasher can refer a friend to dash only after completing at least 30 deliveries and dashing for at least 30 days, and passing quality checks. The bonus is refreshed weekly and varies by city. The referred Dasher must be new, sign up through the link, complete onboarding, and finish the required number of deliveries in the timeframe shown in the app. The bonus is paid to both people and added to weekly pay. No fixed Dasher bonus is published, so check the amount shown in the Dasher app.

If you are a student, the same consumer referral applies to you once you are a DoorDash user. There is no separate student referral amount. See the DoorDash student discount page for the student DashPass offer.

DoorDash Referral Code FAQs

What is a DoorDash referral code?

A DoorDash referral code is a personal referral link in your account, not a typed code. You share the link with someone you know, they sign up through it, and you get credit after their qualifying first order.

How much is the DoorDash referral bonus?

There is no fixed bonus. The amount is shown only in your account and changes often. Published $5, $10, and $15 figures are snapshots from different accounts, not a settled amount for every user.

What does the new user get from a referral?

The new user gets the first-order incentive stated in the referral link. It applies automatically at checkout for their first order, once they meet the requirements in the link.

Can an existing user use a referral link?

No. The referred person must be genuinely new, with no prior DoorDash account and no phone number or card linked to an earlier account. Existing users cannot earn referral credit from other existing users.

Can I use more than one referral link?

No. Only one referral link can be used per new account, and it cannot be added after sign-up.

Can I stack a referral with a promo code?

A referral is not a promo code, so it is not entered at checkout. The new user's referral incentive applies at checkout, and the referrer's credit applies to a later order. Only one promo code can be used per order.

How long does it take to get referral credit?

Referral credit usually appears within 24 hours of the referred person's qualifying order. If 24 hours have not passed yet, wait before checking again.

Do referral credits expire?

Referral credits are subject to the Promotional Offers and Credits terms. Check the expiry shown in your account, because no single expiry is published for every account.

Is there a DoorDash referral code for students?

There is no separate student referral code or amount. Students use the same consumer referral program, and the student saving offer is DashPass for Students. See the DoorDash student discount page.

Is there a referral program for restaurants?

Yes, but it is separate. A referring Marketplace partner gets $1,000 and the new restaurant gets $500, if the restaurant is a small business with 75 stores or fewer, signs up within 30 days, and completes its first 15 orders within 60 days. That is a merchant program, not a customer referral.
