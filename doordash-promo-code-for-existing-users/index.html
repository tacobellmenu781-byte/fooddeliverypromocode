How Do Existing Users Save Without a Promo Code?

Existing users save without a code through the Offers tab, the checkout Promotions panel, merchant promotions and DashPass. Follow this routine in order, and check each place before you pay.

1

Open the DoorDash app and enter your delivery address.

2

Open the Offers tab and check the offers for your address.

3

Open checkout and check Available Rewards and Promotions before you place the order.

4

Open the restaurant page and check its own merchant promotions.

5

Check email and push notifications from DoorDash for targeted offers.

Also check DashPass benefits, pickup, DoubleDash and group orders before you pay.

Is DashPass Worth It for Existing Users?

Yes, DashPass is worth it for existing users who order 2 or more eligible orders per month, and usually not worth it if you order less often. DashPass members get $0 delivery fees, reduced service fees and member only promotions on eligible orders at participating merchants. The benefit applies only when the order meets that merchant's subtotal minimum. DashPass is reported at $9.99 per month or $96 per year, so check the live DashPass page for the current price before you join. DashPass is usually worth it at 2 or more eligible orders per month, and usually not worth it if you order less often, order mostly for pickup, or order below the merchant minimum. DashPass members can also earn 5% back in DoorDash credit on eligible pickup orders.

What Are Win-Back Offers for Lapsed Users?

DoorDash sends targeted offers to accounts that have not ordered for a while, often after 30 or more days. These offers appear in the Offers tab, in email from DoorDash and at checkout, and they are not published on a public code list. If you have not ordered for a while, open the app, check the Offers tab and check the Promotions panel at checkout before you place a new order.
