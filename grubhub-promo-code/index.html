How Do I Enter a Grubhub Promo Code?

Go to checkout, open your bag, and tap "Add gift cards and promo codes" in the payment section. Enter the code exactly as shown, tap Apply, and confirm the total changed before you place the order. A code cannot be added after the order is placed. Only 1 promo code, or 1 discount, works per order.

How Can Existing Users Save on Grubhub?

Existing users have no public sitewide code. Most existing user saving comes from Grubhub+, the Perks tab, restaurant offers, and orders over $50 with no delivery or service fees. Check the Perks tab in the app for restaurant offers for your address, such as BOGO entrees, a free item with a minimum, or a percentage off at a specific restaurant. Those offers are auto-applied at checkout and rotate often. Restaurant orders over $50 have no delivery or service fees for everyone, not just Grubhub+ members.

Is Grubhub+ Worth It?

Grubhub+ costs $9.99 per month plus tax, auto-renewing. It gives $0 delivery fees and lower service fees on eligible orders of $12 or more, 5% back in Grubhub+ credit on eligible pickup orders, discounted priority delivery, and exclusive member offers. The pickup credit expires 90 days after the most recent credit is issued. Grubhub+ is worth it if you order 2 or more eligible deliveries per month, because the delivery fees saved on 2 orders usually cover the $9.99 monthly price. It is usually not worth it if you order less than once a month, order mostly for pickup, or order below the $12 minimum.

Amazon Prime members used to get Grubhub+ free, but that deal ended November 5, 2026, when Amazon made Uber Eats its Prime delivery partner. New Grubhub+ activations through Prime have ended. Members who had already activated Grubhub+ through Prime keep it through December 31, 2027.

How Can I Get Free Delivery on Grubhub?

There is no universal free-delivery code. Free delivery comes from these routes: Grubhub+ with $0 delivery on eligible orders of $12 or more, Grubhub+ Student with free delivery on eligible off-campus orders of $18 or more, restaurant orders over $50 with no delivery or service fees, the $0 delivery filter in the app, and pickup, which has no delivery fee for anyone.

Does Grubhub Have a Student Discount?

Grubhub has no student promo code. The student discount is Grubhub+ Student, free through graduation for active students, faculty, and staff at a campus affiliated with Grubhub Campus Dining. It does not auto-convert into a paid membership. Campus codes FIRSTBITE50 and WELCOME50 are official and campus only, both expiring September 30, 2027.

Does Grubhub Have a Referral Program?

Grubhub referral works by a personal link, not a public code. The referred diner must be new to Grubhub and affiliated brands, and place a first order of $15 or more. The referrer gets a reward by email up to 4 days after the qualifying order, and it normally expires 2 weeks after delivery. The maximum is 20 referrals. The referral amount is not settled: $10 give and get is reported by some sources, $12 by others.
